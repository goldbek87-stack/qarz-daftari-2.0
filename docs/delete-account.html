Ilova ichidan o'chirish (eng tez yo'l)

  1. Qarzdaftarim ilovasini oching va hisobingizga kiring.
  2. Sozlamalar bo'limiga o'ting.
  3. Bulut kartasidagi "Hisobni o'chirish" tugmasini bosing.
  4. Ogohlantirishlarni tasdiqlang va hisob parolini kiriting.

Shundan keyin hisobingiz va bulutdagi hamma ma'lumot (mijozlar, qarzlar, to'lovlar) darhol o'chiriladi. Buni qaytarib bo'lmaydi.

Ilovaga kira olmasangiz

Parolni unutgan yoki ilovani o'chirib yuborgan bo'lsangiz, hisobni o'chirishni goldbek87@gmail.com manziliga so'rang. Xatda hisob loginini yozing. Shaxsingizni tasdiqlash uchun hisobga bog'liq ma'lumot (masalan, mijozlardan birining ismi) so'rashimiz mumkin. So'rov 30 kun ichida bajariladi.

Nima o'chiriladi

  • Hisob (login va parol)
  • Bulutdagi barcha mijozlar, qarz va to'lov yozuvlari

Ilovani telefondan o'chirib tashlasangiz, telefondagi nusxa ham yo'qoladi. Bulutdagi ma'lumot esa faqat yuqoridagi usul bilan o'chiriladi.

Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Qarzdaftarim and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. On the Cloud card tap "Delete account" (Hisobni o'chirish).
  4. Confirm the warnings and enter your account password.

Your account and all data in the cloud (customers, debts, payments) are deleted immediately and this cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in

If you forgot your password or removed the app, request deletion at goldbek87@gmail.com and include your account login. We may ask for account-related details to verify ownership. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The account (login and password)
  • All customers, debt and payment records stored in the cloud

Uninstalling the app removes the copy on your phone; cloud data is removed only by the methods above.
