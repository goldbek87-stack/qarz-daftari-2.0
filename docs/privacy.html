Ilova haqida

Qarzdaftarim — do'kon va tadbirkorlar uchun mijozlar qarzi va to'lovlarini yozib boradigan Android ilovasi. Ilovani Farrux Sadridinov (keyingi o'rinlarda "dasturchi") yaratgan.

Qanday ma'lumotlar yig'iladi

Siz ilovada hisob ochsangiz, quyidagi ma'lumotlar saqlanadi:

  • Hisob ma'lumotlari: siz tanlagan login (yoki Gmail manzil) va parol. Parol Google Firebase Authentication xizmatida xavfsiz (shifrlangan) shaklda saqlanadi, dasturchi uni ko'ra olmaydi.
  • Siz kiritgan yozuvlar: mijozlar ismi, telefon raqami, qarz va to'lov summalari, sana, tovar izohlari, to'lov muddati.

Hisobsiz (faqat telefonda) ishlasangiz, ma'lumotlaringiz faqat qurilmangizda qoladi va hech qayerga yuborilmaydi.

Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Hisob bilan ishlaganda ma'lumotlar telefonda va Google Firebase (Firestore) bulut xizmatida saqlanadi. Ular internet orqali himoyalangan (HTTPS) kanalda uzatiladi. Har bir hisob faqat o'z ma'lumotini ko'ra oladi. Bir loginga bir nechta kishi kirsa, ular umumiy daftarni ko'radi.

Kimlar ko'ra oladi

  • Siz va shu login-parol bilan kirgan odamlar.
  • Dasturchi Firebase boshqaruv panelida ma'lumotlarga texnik jihatdan kira oladi, lekin ularni faqat nosozlikni tuzatish yoki qonun talabi bo'lganda ko'radi. Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.
  • Google (Firebase) xizmati ma'lumotlarni bizning nomimizdan saqlaydi. Uning qoidalari: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Mikrofon va ovozli kiritish

Ovoz bilan yozish uchun ilova mikrofon ruxsatini so'raydi. Ovozni matnga aylantirishni Android tizimining nutqni tanish xizmati (odatda Google) bajaradi. Ilovaning o'zi ovozni yozib olmaydi va saqlamaydi. Ruxsat bermasangiz, ilova qo'lda kiritish bilan ishlaydi.

Bildirishnomalar

To'lov muddati kelganda eslatma ko'rsatish uchun ilova telefoningizning o'zida bildirishnoma yaratadi. Bu eslatmalar serverlar orqali yuborilmaydi.

PDF hisobot va zahira nusxa

Fayllar faqat siz so'raganingizda yaratiladi. Qayerga saqlash yoki kimga yuborishni Android'ning ulashish oynasida o'zingiz tanlaysiz.

Reklama va kuzatuv

Ilovada reklama, tahlil (analytics) va kuzatuv vositalari yo'q.

Mijozlaringiz ma'lumoti

Siz kiritadigan mijozlar ma'lumoti (ism, telefon, qarz) boshqa odamlarga tegishli. Ularni faqat qonuniy maqsadda (o'z hisobingiz uchun) kiriting.

Ma'lumotlarni saqlash va o'chirish

  • Ma'lumotlar siz o'chirmaguncha saqlanadi. O'chirilgan mijoz yoki yozuv "O'chirilganlar" bo'limida 30 kun turadi, keyin butunlay o'chadi.
  • Hisobni va barcha ma'lumotni ilova ichidan o'chirish mumkin: Sozlamalar → Bulut → "Hisobni o'chirish". Batafsil: Hisobni o'chirish sahifasi.

Bolalar

Ilova 18 yoshdan kichik bolalar uchun mo'ljallanmagan va ularning ma'lumotini ongli ravishda yig'maydi.

About the app

Qarzdaftarim is an Android app for shops and small businesses to record customers' debts and payments. It is developed by Farrux Sadridinov ("the developer").

Summary

If you create an account, your data is stored on your phone and in Google Firebase cloud. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and the app has no ads or tracking. If you use the app without an account, data stays only on your device.

Data collected

  • Account data: the login (or Gmail address) and password you choose. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication in protected form; the developer cannot see it.
  • Records you enter: customer names, phone numbers, debt and payment amounts, dates, item notes, due dates.

Where data is stored

With an account, data is stored on the device and in Google Firebase (Firestore) and transmitted over HTTPS. Each account can read only its own data. If several people sign in with the same login, they see the same ledger.

Who can access it

  • You and anyone who signs in with the same login and password.
  • The developer can technically access data through the Firebase console, but does so only to fix technical faults or when required by law.
  • Google (Firebase) stores the data on our behalf, see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Microphone and voice input

For voice entry the app asks for microphone permission. Speech is converted to text by the Android system speech recognition service (usually Google). The app itself does not record or store audio. Without the permission, the app works with manual entry.

Notifications

Due-date reminders are created locally on your device and are not sent through servers.

PDF reports and backups

Files are created only when you request them. You choose where to save them or whom to send them to in Android's share dialog.

Ads and tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics, or tracking tools.

Your customers' data

Customer data you enter belongs to other people. Enter it only for lawful purposes related to your own bookkeeping.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete it. Deleted customers or records stay in the in-app "Deleted items" list for 30 days and are then permanently removed.
  • You can delete your account and all data inside the app: Settings → Cloud → "Delete account". Details: account deletion page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will be shown at the top of this page.
